Send to the GanderData database

GanderData is the shared database behind the Gander addon: what each mob casts, and its stats. This page reads your combat logs and sends what mobs cast in them.

Your logs stay on this computer. The page reads them here, in your browser. Only mob names, spell names, counts and the time spent in each dungeon are sent. No player's name is.

1. Choose your combat logs

Drop your Logs folder or log files here

The folder is World of Warcraft\_forever_\Logs for Forever and World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Logs for Classic Era. The game only writes a log while logging is on: tick it in Gander's settings, or type /combatlog.

    2. Send

    Sending needs a GitHub account for now. For each block: copy it, open the form, paste it into the Data box and submit.

    Wakes up once every block has been copied.

    Thank you. This browser will skip those logs next time, so you can drop the whole folder again after your next session.
