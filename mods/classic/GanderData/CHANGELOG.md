## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release.

- The database for the Gander addon, as its own addon. Gander reads it when it's installed: tooltips and the bestiary start from what's in here, and anything you see yourself still wins.
- Holds what mobs cast (from combat log files, which is the only way to know on World of Warcraft: Forever) and the stats players have seen.
- Starts with Blackfathom Deeps and the Barrens on Forever.
- Every later version is new data only.
