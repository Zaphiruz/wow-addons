# Bushel changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

- Counts every herb you gather, vein you mine and creature you skin, by type, for each character and your whole account.
- Hover over a node or creature to see its count in the tooltip, such as "Mined: 37 (account 212)".
- A vein mined several times counts once. Gathers that fail or are interrupted don't count.
- `/bushel list` prints this character's counts, most gathered first. `/bushel debug` prints each gather as it's counted.
- Works on Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever.
