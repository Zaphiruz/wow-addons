# Storied changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-11)

- Type `/storied` to open your story: a window listing your level-ups and deaths, newest first, with filters for each. `/storied settings` opens the settings.
- Level-ups record where you dinged, your total time played, and how long the level took. Storied asks for /played quietly, so it doesn't fill your chat.
- Deaths record what killed you (and with which spell), where, at what level, and how big your group was. On World of Warcraft: Forever it names the enemy you were fighting.
- Your story starts the first time you log in with this version; earlier levels and deaths can't be recovered.
- New switches for "Level-ups" and "Deaths" in Options > AddOns > Storied.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-11)

- Players you've grouped with now have a history on their tooltip: where you first and last grouped, and how many times, such as "First grouped: Scholomance, 3 Oct 2026".
- A group counts once when someone joins it, at the dungeon it goes into. A different dungeon with the same group is a new time; running back in after a wipe isn't.
- Their tooltip also says when they resurrected, summoned or soulstoned you, once you accepted it. A soulstone can only be credited when its caster was in your group.
- Optional thank-you whisper when you accept a resurrect or summon. It's off until you switch on "Say thanks" in the settings, where both messages can be edited.
- New switches for "Grouped with" and "Help" in Options > AddOns > Storied.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- Remembers the history of your gear and shows it in item tooltips. It records from the day you install it; nothing earlier can be recovered.
- Enchants: who put each enchant on your gear and when, from the "Will not be traded" slot of a trade, such as "Crusader by Bob, 11 Nov 2026". Once an enchant is replaced, the old one isn't shown.
- Where it came from, for green and better gear: the creature and zone it dropped in (and how many were in your group), chests, lockboxes (with who picked the lock), quest rewards, vendors, trades and mail, including auctions you won.
- Weapon kills: your killing blows, counted on the weapon in your hand, or on your bow, gun or wand right after a shot.
- Each part can be switched off in Options > AddOns > Storied, or type `/storied`. "Show on tooltips" hides the lines without stopping the recording.
- History is kept per character, and per kind of item: two copies of the same item share one history.
- Works on Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever.
