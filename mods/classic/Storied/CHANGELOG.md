# Storied changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- Remembers the history of your gear and shows it in item tooltips. It records from the day you install it; nothing earlier can be recovered.
- Enchants: who put each enchant on your gear and when, from the "Will not be traded" slot of a trade, such as "Crusader by Bob, 11 Nov 2026". Once an enchant is replaced, the old one isn't shown.
- Where it came from, for green and better gear: the creature and zone it dropped in (and how many were in your group), chests, lockboxes (with who picked the lock), quest rewards, vendors, trades and mail, including auctions you won.
- Weapon kills: your killing blows, counted on the weapon in your hand, or on your bow, gun or wand right after a shot.
- Each part can be switched off in Options > AddOns > Storied, or type `/storied`. "Show on tooltips" hides the lines without stopping the recording.
- History is kept per character, and per kind of item: two copies of the same item share one history.
- Works on Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever.
