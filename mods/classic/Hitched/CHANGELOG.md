# Hitched changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-26)

- First version. Target a player and type `/hitched propose`: you go down on one knee, and they get a question with "I do" and "No". Both of you need Hitched.
- A yes marries your two characters. They say yes with an emote, you cheer, and guild and group members who have Hitched cheer for you too.
- Married players show "Married to ..." on their tooltip for everyone with Hitched. Your own spouse's tooltip says since when.
- `/hitched divorce` ends a marriage, after asking if you're sure. Your spouse is told at once if they're online, or the next time you meet.
- `/hitched` says who you're married to. Options > AddOns > Hitched has switches for the tooltip line, cheering for other weddings, and letting players propose to you.
- With the Storied addon, your proposals, weddings and divorces appear in your story.
- Works on Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever.
